# Xiara Acosta | LMS, Course Content, and Digital Learning Assets

**View the live portfolio: [xiaraacosta.com](https://xiaraacosta.com/)**

I build and maintain the online courses, web tutorials, videos, and digital files that learners depend on, and I make sure every piece is accurate, accessible, and easy to find. For more than 13 years at Nova Southeastern University (NSU), I have set up Canvas courses, prepared and published media, managed repository collections, and trained students, faculty, and staff. I am completing an M.S. in Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence (December 2026), where I work with SQL, Python, Tableau, and Git.

This repository holds the source for my portfolio site, published with GitHub Pages.

## What's in the portfolio

### LMS administration and course builds
- **Learning SciVal: Faculty Development in Canvas.** A self-paced, six-module Canvas course that teaches NSU faculty to explore research activity, interpret scholarly metrics, and communicate research impact. Adapted from Elsevier's SciVal training and customized for NSU. *In development, launching January 2027.*
- **UNIV 1000 Course Setup in Canvas.** Built the course structure, configured the gradebook (assignment groups, weights, grading schemes, rubrics), and reviewed the course against the syllabus.

### Digital repository
- **NSUWorks (bepress Digital Commons).** Upload and manage items, set up collections and journals, and help authors submit their work.

### Web-based learning tools
- **[Evaluate Sources with CARDIO](https://xiaraacosta.com/CARDIO/).** A self-paced tutorial that teaches computing students to judge source credibility using six criteria, with scaffolded practice, reflection prompts, and knowledge checks aligned to a graded course quiz.
- **[Database Keyword Search Builder](https://xiaraacosta.com/asl-database-search-builder/).** A six-step guided tool, with JavaScript interactivity, that turns a vague research idea into ready-to-paste database search strings.

### Learner guides
- [Basic Look at Video Editing](https://libguides.nova.edu/videoedit), a beginner's guide to iMovie and Adobe Premiere Rush that grew into a recurring class workshop
- [College of Computing, AI, and Cybersecurity](https://libguides.nova.edu/ccac), the subject guide I maintain as library liaison

### Visual and print design
- **Library of Things: Collectible Bookmark Campaign.** Six numbered bookmarks, illustrated with AI image tools under my creative direction, that use light gamification to promote borrowable items and send patrons to three library service points to complete the set.
- **Online Resources Bookmark.** A two-sided print job aid introducing the library's digital reading and learning collections.
