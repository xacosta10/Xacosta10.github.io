# Xiara Acosta | Instructional Design Portfolio

**View the live portfolio: [xiaraacosta.com](https://xiaraacosta.com/)**

I design self-paced tutorials, software how-to videos, workshops, and learner guides that make complex tools and research skills easy to learn. For more than 13 years at Nova Southeastern University (NSU), I have taught students, faculty, and community learners in person, online, and in hybrid formats. I am completing an M.S. in Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence (December 2026).

This repository holds the source for my portfolio site, published with GitHub Pages.

## What's in the portfolio

### Canvas course design
- **Learning SciVal: Faculty Development in Canvas.** A self-paced, six-module Canvas course that teaches NSU faculty to explore research activity, interpret scholarly metrics, and communicate research impact. Adapted from Elsevier's SciVal training and customized for NSU. *In development, launching January 2027.*

### Interactive e-learning
- **[Evaluate Sources with CARDIO](https://xiaraacosta.com/CARDIO/).** A self-paced tutorial that teaches computing students to judge source credibility using six criteria, with scaffolded practice, reflection prompts, and knowledge checks aligned to a graded course quiz.
- **[Database Keyword Search Builder](https://xiaraacosta.com/asl-database-search-builder/).** A six-step guided tool that turns a vague research idea into ready-to-paste database search strings.

### Software how-to videos
Short, captioned screencasts that I script, record, and edit:
- [How to Use Advanced Search in the Catalog](https://nsuworks.nova.edu/library_learn_all/11/)
- [How to Reserve a Study Room](https://nsuworks.nova.edu/library_learn_all/133/)
- [How to Search for Articles](https://nsuworks.nova.edu/library_learn_all/49/) (co-created)

### Workshops and presentations
- [The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly of AI](https://nsuworks.nova.edu/library_learn_all/135/), a student AI literacy workshop
- [Narrative Data Visualization in Academia & Libraries](https://nsuworks.nova.edu/library_learn_all/129/)
- [AI Assistants in Research Databases](https://nsuworks.nova.edu/library_learn_all/116/) (co-presented)
- **English Café**, a recurring conversation program I created for adult speakers of other languages
- **Online library workshops** for the College of Computing and Engineering: Introduction to the Library and Accessing Dissertations

### Learner guides
- [Basic Look at Video Editing](https://libguides.nova.edu/videoedit), a beginner's guide to iMovie and Adobe Premiere Rush that grew into a recurring class workshop
- [College of Computing, AI, and Cybersecurity](https://libguides.nova.edu/ccac), the subject guide I maintain as library liaison

### Visual and print design
- **Library of Things: Collectible Bookmark Campaign.** Six numbered, illustrated bookmarks that use light gamification to promote borrowable items and send patrons to three library service points to complete the set.
- **Online Resources Bookmark.** A two-sided print job aid introducing the library's digital reading and learning collections.
